Frequently asked questions

How do I sign up as a Glenerds affiliate?
Visit any Glenerds product page on Gumroad and look for the affiliate / “become an affiliate” option. Gumroad runs the affiliate program itself: you get a personal share link, and Gumroad handles the tracking and the payouts. Start here: glenerds.gumroad.com/affiliates
How much commission do affiliates earn?
30% commission on every paid product sale made through your affiliate link. Every product is a one-time purchase, so each sale pays out in full — no recurring-revenue math.
How and when do I get paid?
Gumroad handles affiliate tracking and payouts directly. Payment timing and thresholds are set by Gumroad's affiliate terms, which you will see when you sign up through a product page.
What can I promote?
All ten paid Glenerds products — the full list is below, from the $9 MoveAudit scam checker to the $29 coaching, BBQ, boating, and inspection toolkits. Promote one niche or all of them.
Do I need a website or a big audience?
No. Share your affiliate link anywhere your audience already is: a blog, a YouTube description, a newsletter, a coaching community, a landlord forum. Relevance beats reach — a small audience of flippers will outperform a large generic one.
Can I promote the free tools too?
Yes — and you should. The free calculators and checklists are a natural top of funnel: readers try the free tool, trust the quality, and buy the paid toolkit when they need the full version. Commissions apply to paid sales.
Are there rules about how I promote?
Be honest: describe the tools accurately, do not invent features or prices, and disclose the affiliate relationship wherever you post links. Do not spam, and do not bid on the Glenerds brand name in paid ads.
