Frequently asked questions

How many bids can I compare?
Up to three per project, which covers the standard get-three-bids workflow most investors use.
What counts as a material price difference?
The tool flags line items where one bid diverges meaningfully from the others, so you know exactly which numbers to question.
Can I print the report?
Yes — the report tab is built to be printed or saved, with talking points for the negotiation call.
Does it estimate ARV for me?
You enter your ARV; the tool projects profit against it so each bid is judged in the context of the deal.
