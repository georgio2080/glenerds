How embedding works

Each snippet is a plain <iframe> pointing at the tool's embed page on this site. Paste it into any HTML page, blog post, or CMS that accepts raw HTML. The tool keeps working even if your page is viewed offline after first load, and every embed carries a discreet “Powered by Glenerds” link back to the full free tool. Suggested heights are starting points — adjust the height attribute if your layout needs more or less room.
