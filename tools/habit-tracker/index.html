How to use the habit tracker

Add each habit you want to build — exercise, reading, meditation, saving money, anything with a daily repetition. Each habit gets a row of the last 14 days. Click a date to mark it done; click again to undo. Your current streak counts backward from today, so a missed day simply resets the counter without erasing your history. Use the export button any time to download a JSON backup of all your habits, which you can keep with your other files or move to another browser.

Why streaks work

Habit research consistently finds that visible progress is one of the strongest motivators for keeping a new behavior going. A streak turns an abstract goal ("exercise more") into a concrete daily question ("did I keep the chain alive today?"). The tracker above is deliberately minimal: no gamified coins, no social feed, no reminders begging for attention — just your habits and your history. That simplicity is also why it works offline: there is no server to sync with, so the whole tool is a single page that loads instantly and works on a plane, in a basement, or anywhere else with no signal.

Worked example

Add a habit called "Read 20 pages". Tap today's dot, then the 13 dots before it. The tracker reads Current streak: 14 days with 14 total check-ins. Miss tomorrow and the streak resets to zero — but your history stays, so those 14 days still count in your totals. That's the whole game: the streak is the motivator, the history is the record.

Frequently asked questions

Is this habit tracker really free?
Yes. Every feature on this page — unlimited habits, streak counts, totals, and JSON export — is free and always will be. There is no premium tier and no account wall.
Where is my data stored?
In your browser's local storage, on your device only. Glenerds never sees it, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data will erase your habits, so use the export button for backups you care about.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The page is responsive and works in any modern mobile browser. Add it to your home screen for app-like access; it still runs fully offline after the first load.
Can I track weekly instead of daily habits?
The tracker is built around daily check-ins, which is where streaks are most motivating. For a weekly habit, many people check in on the day they do it and simply let the other days stay empty — the total check-in count still tells the real story.
What if I want more than a habit tracker?
If you coach clients or run a fitness business, the free tracker above covers personal habits. For managing other people's check-ins — weekly logs, photo protocols, measurements, and client feedback drafts — take a look at the paid toolkit below.
