Your landlord raised the rent. Should you stay?

A renewal offer is never just the new rent number. Moving has a real price tag — movers, a new security deposit, application fees, days of double rent, utility setups, cleaning the old place — and staying has a hidden one: every extra dollar of rent, times every month you're locked in.

Most tenants compare monthly rent vs monthly rent and stop there. That's the wrong comparison. The right one is total cost over the lease term, including every one-time moving cost:

  • Staying: horizon × your renewal rent.
  • Moving: horizon × the rent at a new place plus everything you pay once to move.

Enter your numbers below and get a clear verdict: which option is cheaper over your comparison period, a line-by-line breakdown, and the break-even month — the point where moving's upfront costs finally pay off (if they ever do).
