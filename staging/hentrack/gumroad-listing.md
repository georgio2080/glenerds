## Description
**What does your flock really cost you?** HenTrack is the offline egg & flock tracker for backyard chicken keepers — log daily egg counts, feed bags and supply costs, and it works out your true cost per dozen automatically.

**What's inside:**
- 🥚 Daily egg log — counts, damaged eggs, notes (double yolks, first eggs, molting dips)
- 🐔 Flock records — names, breeds, hatch dates, laying/molting/broody status
- 💰 Feed & cost tracker — feed, bedding, supplements, vet, equipment, categorized
- 📊 Automatic math — lay rate %, cost per dozen (trailing 30 days), 14-day egg chart
- 💵 Egg sales log — dozens sold, revenue, all-time profit vs. spend
- 🌙 Dark mode, works on phone/tablet/computer

**Private by design:** 100% offline single HTML file. No account, no subscription, no tracking — your flock data never leaves your device. Export a JSON backup any time.
