## Description

**Stop guessing what your remodel actually costs.**

If you're mid-renovation and tracking costs in a spreadsheet you dread opening —
or worse, in your head — RenoTrack is the offline budget tracker built for
real job sites. One file, no account, no subscription, no internet required.
Open it on your phone at the property with zero bars and it just works.

**What it does for you:**

- **See your whole remodel budget at a glance** — total budget, what's spent,
  what's committed, what's left, and % used, on one dashboard.
- **Track every dollar by category** — 13 renovation-ready categories
  (Demolition, Plumbing, Electrical, Kitchen, Flooring, Labor…) with budgeted
  vs. actual progress bars. Over-budget categories light up red before they
  snowball.
- **Compare contractor quotes side by side** — store up to 4 bids per job,
  see the price range instantly, and mark the winner. Accepted bids count as
  committed spend so you never double-book your budget.
- **Log change orders properly** — every "while you're at it…" gets a cost
  impact and a proposed / approved / rejected status. Approved ones roll into
  your actuals automatically — no more mystery overruns.
- **Contingency guard** — set your contingency as a % of budget and get
  warned the moment change orders start eating it (amber at 75%, red when
  it's gone).
- **Multiple projects** — kitchen remodel, bath flip, whole-house — each with
  its own budgets, expenses, quotes, and change orders.
- **Works fully offline** — a single HTML file. No spreadsheet skills needed,
  no cloud, no monthly fee. Your data stays on your device (localStorage) with
  one-click JSON backup export/import.
- **Dark mode, mobile-friendly, printable** — clean on a 360px phone screen,
  easy on the eyes after hours, and prints a tidy budget summary for your
  records or your partner.
- **One-time purchase** — pay once, use it forever. No subscription, no monthly
  fee, free updates for life.
