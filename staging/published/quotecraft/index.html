Discount & tax

Totals

Exact-to-the-cent: every amount is computed in whole cents (integers) — no floating-point drift. Tax-after-discount rule: tax applies only to taxable lines, after the discount. The discount is split between taxable and non-taxable lines in proportion to each side’s share of the subtotal; tax = (taxable subtotal − its share of the discount) × tax rate, rounded to the cent.
