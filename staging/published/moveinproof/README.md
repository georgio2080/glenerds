# MoveInProof — Apartment Move-In Damage Logger

Document your apartment's condition on move-in day, room by room — with photos —
before you unpack. Prove pre-existing damage and protect your security deposit.

## What's inside
- `index.html` — the complete app. Single file, works offline.
  Open it in any modern browser (double-click, or File → Open). No install,
  no account, no internet needed — built for move-in day on your phone.
- `gallery/` — 8 screenshots showing the app in action.

## Features
- Property details: address, landlord, tenants, lease start, move-in date
- Room-by-room condition log: rating (Excellent/Good/Fair/Poor) + notes
- Defect entries: item, defect type, severity, description, auto timestamp
- Photo documentation: attach photos to rooms and defects (auto-downscaled,
  stored privately in your browser)
- Printable handover report with tenant/landlord signature blocks
- JSON backup export/import, demo data to explore
- Dark mode (follows your system, remembers your choice)
- Everything stays in your browser — your data never leaves your device
