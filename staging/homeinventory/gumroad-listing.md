## Description
**If fire, flood, or theft hit tomorrow, could you list everything you own for your insurance claim — with purchase values, serial numbers, and photos?** Most people can't — and scrambling to reconstruct that list after a loss is exactly what slows claims down. This free home inventory app fixes it: catalog your belongings room by room with a room-by-room checklist, then print a clean, print-friendly report with serial numbers and values you can hand to your adjuster.

**What's inside:**
- 🏠 Room-by-room catalog — each room shows its item count and total value
- 📝 Item records — name, category, purchase value, serial number, purchase date, note, and one photo per item
- 📊 Live totals + charts — per-room and grand totals computed as you type, plus value-by-room and value-by-category visuals
- 🖨️ Print-friendly claim report — a room-by-room table with serial numbers and values you can hand to your adjuster; printing outputs only the report
- 🔍 Search + room filter — find any item by name, serial, note, category, or room
- 💾 JSON backup export/import — your backup file includes everything, photos included
- 🌙 Dark mode — works on phone, tablet, or computer

**Private by design:** a single HTML file (delivered as a ZIP), 100% offline. No account, no ads, no subscription, no tracking — your belongings data never leaves your device. Free forever.

**How it works:** download the ZIP, unzip it, and open the HTML file in any browser — on your phone, tablet, or computer. (On a phone, unzip the file first, then open the HTML file in your browser.) No install, no server, no account needed. Your data stays saved in your browser automatically.

**⚠️ Back up your inventory:** your data is stored in your browser — clearing your browser's site data will erase it. Use Export JSON and keep the backup file somewhere safe. One device per copy: it's fully offline, so there's no sync between devices — pick one device as your master.

**Good to know:** the tool records what you paid for each item; your insurer determines the actual claim value. For personal record-keeping only — not insurance or legal advice.
