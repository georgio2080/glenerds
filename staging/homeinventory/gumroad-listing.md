## Description
**If fire, flood, or theft hit tomorrow, could you list everything you own for your insurance claim — with purchase values, serial numbers, and photos?** Most people can't, and that gap costs them thousands at claim time. HomeInventory fixes it: catalog your belongings room by room, then print a claim-ready insurance report in minutes.

**What's inside:**
- 🏠 Room-by-room catalog — rooms with item counts and total documented value each
- 📝 Item records — name, category, purchase value, serial number, purchase date, notes, and a photo per item
- 📊 Live totals — per-room and grand totals computed as you type
- 🖨️ Claim-ready report — room-by-room table with serial #s and values; printing outputs only the report, ready for your adjuster
- 🔍 Search + room filter — find any item by name, serial, note, category, or room
- 💾 JSON backup export/import
- 🌙 Dark mode, works on phone/tablet/computer

**Private by design:** 100% offline single HTML file. No account, no ads, no subscription, no tracking — your belongings data never leaves your device. Free forever.

**How it works:** download the ZIP, unzip it, and open the HTML file in any browser — on your phone, tablet, or computer. No install, no server, no account needed. Your data stays saved in your browser automatically.
