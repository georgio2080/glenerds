An HOA Management Software Alternative — for Buyers, Not Managers

HOA management software is subscription-based and built for the people who run associations: boards and management companies collecting dues, tracking violations, running resident portals, and doing the accounting. If you are buying a condo, you are not managing the HOA — you are deciding whether its finances are sound enough to buy into.

Where HOA management software win

  • You sit on the board or manage the HOA: dues collection, communications, and accounting are daily work.
  • You need a resident portal, violation tracking, and maintenance requests.
  • The association needs ongoing operational software, not a one-time analysis.

Where HOA ReserveCheck wins

  • You are buying, not managing: one analysis during due diligence, not a monthly platform.
  • $9 once versus a subscription built for an organization.

The short version: Pick HOA management software if you run the association. Pick HOA ReserveCheck if you are buying into one and need to know whether the finances hold up.

Frequently asked questions

Is HOA ReserveCheck a replacement for HOA management software?
No — different audience entirely. Management software serves boards and managers running the association; ReserveCheck serves buyers evaluating it once, before purchase.
What documents do I need?
The HOA resale package and reserve study. The built-in 12-item checklist tells you exactly what to request if anything is missing.
What is a special assessment?
A one-time charge owners pay when reserves cannot cover a major repair. The likelihood gauge estimates how probable one is from reserve funding levels.
Is this financial or legal advice?
No. It is a record-keeping and analysis tool that organizes the documents' figures into a clear risk picture. Your agent, lender, or attorney makes the call.
Can I estimate an assessment free?
Yes. The free HOA special assessment calculator estimates your personal share from project cost, unit count, and reserves — and shows what it equals in months of dues.
