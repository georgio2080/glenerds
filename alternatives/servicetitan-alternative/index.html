A ServiceTitan Alternative for Investors Who Just Need to Compare Bids

ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro are contractor management suites: subscription-based platforms built for contracting businesses to run dispatch, scheduling, CRM, and invoicing. If you are a house flipper or investor who hires contractors but does not run a contracting company, that is a lot of software for one job: comparing the bids in front of you.

Try it free: free contractor bid comparison worksheet  ·  Full version: FlipBid Reconciler ($29 one-time)

Contractor management suites are excellent at what they are built for: running a contracting business. Dispatching technicians, scheduling jobs, managing customer relationships, sending invoices, and keeping a whole crew coordinated. They are typically subscription-based and priced for businesses with teams to manage.

FlipBid Reconciler does a different, much smaller job: you receive contractor bids, and it helps you compare them. Up to three bids go into one normalized scope-of-work matrix. Line items where one contractor's price diverges materially from the others get flagged. Work a contractor did not bid at all gets called out as a scope hole. The report tab turns it into a negotiation-ready variance report with per-contractor totals and projected profit against your ARV.

Neither replaces the other. The fair question is which job you actually need done.

Where Contractor management suites win

  • You run a contracting business: dispatch, scheduling, technician management, and CRM are the core of the suite.
  • You need client-facing features: online booking, customer notifications, and invoicing.
  • You want integrations with accounting and payroll systems as the company scales.

Where FlipBid Reconciler wins

  • You receive bids and need to compare them, not send them.
  • $29 once beats a monthly subscription for a job you do a few times a year.
  • Offline single file: your deal numbers never leave your device, and there is no account to manage.
  • Purpose-built scope-hole detection: it calls out work a contractor did not bid at all.

The short version: Pick a contractor management suite if you operate the contracting business. Pick FlipBid Reconciler if you are the one hiring contractors and want every bid judged apples-to-apples against the deal.

Frequently asked questions

Is FlipBid a replacement for ServiceTitan?
No, and it does not try to be. Management suites run a contracting business; FlipBid compares the bids you receive as a buyer of contracting work. Different jobs, different buyers.
Do I need a subscription to use FlipBid?
No. It is a $29 one-time purchase. The tool is a single file that runs offline in your browser.
How many bids can I compare?
Up to three per project, which covers the standard get-three-bids workflow most investors use.
I am a contractor. Should I use FlipBid to send bids?
It is built for the bid-receiving side. Contractors sending estimates will get more from their trade's estimating and management tools.
Can I try the comparison approach free first?
Yes. The free contractor bid comparison worksheet lines up two bids, flags gaps over 15%, and spots missing scope. FlipBid adds the third bid, the variance report, and profit vs. ARV.
