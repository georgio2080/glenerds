A Trainerize / TrueCoach Alternative for Coaches Who Just Need Check-Ins

Trainerize and TrueCoach are subscription-based all-in-one coaching platforms: client mobile apps, workout and nutrition program delivery, messaging, and billing in one system. If your actual weekly grind is simpler — clients send check-ins, you review them and write feedback — you may be paying for a client app your clients barely open.

Try it free: free fitness client check-in tracker  ·  Full version: FitnessCheckIn ($29 one-time)

Full coaching platforms earn their subscription when you deliver programs through them: clients log workouts in the app, follow the nutrition plan, message you inside the system, and pay through the platform. That is a real workflow, and the platforms are built around it.

FitnessCheckIn covers the other workflow: the weekly check-in. One offline home for your whole roster. Log each client's check-in — weight, measurements, macros vs. actuals, adherence scores, photo protocol checklists — compare any two weeks side by side to spot trends, and turn the numbers into a feedback message draft with one click. Your clients change nothing: they send check-ins however they already do, and there is nothing for them to install.

Twenty-nine dollars once. No per-client pricing, no subscription, no client accounts — and because it is a single offline file, client data never leaves your device.
