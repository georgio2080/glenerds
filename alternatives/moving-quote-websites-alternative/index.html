Moving quote websites are typically free to use and earn through partnerships with movers: you enter your details, you get ballpark estimates, and movers may contact you with quotes. They solve the “find movers” problem. They do not solve the “is this mover legitimate” problem — that part is still on you.

Frequently asked questions

Are moving quote websites scams?
Not as a category — most are legitimate lead and quote services. The risk is not the website; it is individual rogue movers, which is exactly what MoveAudit screens for.
What is the federal 110% rule?
On interstate moves with a non-binding estimate, the mover generally cannot demand more than 110% of the estimate at delivery. MoveAudit checks your quotes against it.
What are the 35 red-flag signals?
The most common moving scam patterns: suspiciously low estimates, large deposit demands, name mismatches on credentials, no physical address, and dozens more, each weighted into a risk score.
