A Craftybase Alternative for Makers Who Just Need Pricing

Craftybase is a subscription-based inventory management and pricing service for makers: it tracks raw materials, stock levels, and production alongside pricing. If your actual problem is simpler — “your prices do not cover your costs and you are not sure” — you may not need the inventory half at all.

  • You want production planning and material reordering tied to your sales.
  • You sell across channels and want inventory synced in one system.

Where CraftCost wins

  • Your problem is pricing, not inventory: $14 once, no monthly fee for modules you will not use.

The short version: Pick Craftybase-style software when inventory management is the job. Pick CraftCost when the job is pricing your handmade goods so every sale actually makes money.
